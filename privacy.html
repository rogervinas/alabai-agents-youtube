Privacy policy

Alabai Agents YouTube, a Google app of Alabai Agents. Last updated 2 November 2026.

Alabai Agents YouTube is a private tool, not offered as a public service. This page explains what it accesses through Google and what happens to that data.

What the app accesses

With the permission of the YouTube channel's owner (the Google scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube), the tool uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • read the channel's details and its list of videos, with their status, schedule and view counts;
  • upload videos, and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy and publishing time;
  • add videos to the channel's playlists;
  • read the comments on the channel's videos.

It only acts on the channel of the account that signed in, and only when that account's owner runs it.

Where the data goes

  • The tool runs locally. There is no server and no database.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in local secure storage.
  • Data read from YouTube is shown to the owner and not stored anywhere else.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Alabai Agents YouTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (“Alabai Agents YouTube”). The tool then can no longer reach the channel.
