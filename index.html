Alabai Agents YouTube

Alabai Agents YouTube is a private tool that lets the owner of a YouTube channel manage it from their own computer, through the YouTube Data API: list the channel's videos and their statistics, upload new videos, edit titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, schedule when videos go public, organize playlists and read comments.

It only acts on the channel of the account that signs in, and only when its owner runs it. It is not a public service, and there is nothing to sign up for.
